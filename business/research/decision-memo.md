## 0. Summary

1. **Gate 0 (before any sale): legal status.** The owner must be lawfully able to be self-employed in Canada, whether as a citizen, a permanent resident, or on a permit that allows self-employment. The research did not cover which permits allow this ("Work authorization … NOT FOUND. Owner must confirm their own eligibility for self-employment", labour dossier). The owner confirms it from their own IRCC documents. **If the owner is not authorized, this plan does not apply.**
2. **Primary business: one-brand residential home services, sold street by street in one neighbourhood cluster.**
   - **Track A always runs.** It covers flat-rate deep cleans and move-in/move-out cleans, then conversion of those clients to recurring cleaning.
   - **Track B (exterior) is an add-on that switches on only if Gate G1 passes.** Gate G1 is defined in section 2. Track B has two parts:
     - Gutter/eavestrough cleaning, **gutters only** (no windows, no pressure washing), in the late-fall window.
     - Walk-behind or shovel snow contracts, sold in Oct–Nov with **no payment taken before Dec 1**, billed in 4 monthly instalments (Dec 1 – Mar 1). Each contract is void unless the break-even number of contracts is signed by Nov 20.
3. **Parallel cash-flow floor:**
   - **EI** (if eligible): file **by Sat Oct 10, 2026** (F1).
   - **Decide Path A vs Path B with the explicit rule in section 5.4.** Path A means staying on EI with a small, declared side activity. Path B means running the full business with no EI.
   - **Apply to TaskRabbit and Jiffy on day 1.** How much work a new Tasker gets from TaskRabbit is **not found** (labour verifier), and approval time is **not found**. Platform income is a hedge, not a guarantee.
4. **Do not do:** standalone holiday lights, paid immigration or "paperwork" help, rideshare as a $5k plan, Amazon arbitrage or pallets, content/affiliate, SaaS, freelance marketplaces as the primary, pressure washing in Toronto, truck plowing, lump-sum snow prepayment. The reasons are in section 3.
5. **Honest expectation (section 5, all ESTIMATE):**
   - No scenario averages $5,000/month net over October–November.
   - **Base case without the exterior add-on (B1):** about $5,070/month is first reached in **January 2027**.
   - **Base case with the exterior add-on (B2):** operating net is about $7,100 in November. After one-time snow costs, cash is about $4,810 in November and about $5,390 in December.
   - **The conservative cases never reach $5,000/month solo this winter.** They sit around $2,100–2,700/month.
   - **If the owner qualifies for EI at the maximum rate,** Path A (about $929/week in the example) pays more than the business in October under every scenario.
   - The verifiers' own probability scores for averaging $5k within about 60 days top out at 3/10 (fall exterior) and 2.5/10 (cleaning).

## 2. Final choice

### Gates (decided in week 1–2)

- **Gate 0: work authorization.** Owner confirms, day 1. If the owner is not authorized, stop.
- **Gate G1: exterior add-on.** All five conditions must hold:
  1. A vehicle that can carry a 24–28 ft extension ladder.
  2. The owner is fit and comfortable on a ladder.
  3. A broker has **confirmed in writing** that the CGL policy covers ladder/eavestrough work.
  4. Money for the ladder, the insurance and the $1,500 setup (A5).
  5. **At least 5 gutter jobs booked with dates** before the ladder is bought.
- **G1 timing:** decided by **Mon Oct 5 or when the written broker confirmation arrives, whichever is later**. Hard stop **Fri Oct 9**: if G1 has not passed by then, gutters are dropped for 2026. The peak window is too short to recover the setup cost (break-even about 10 jobs, section 5.3).
- **Gate S (snow, separate):** written snow cover **before any snow contract is signed**, target by Fri Oct 30. Contracts become binding only if the break-even count is signed by Nov 20 (section 5.3). **Car-less owners may sell shovel-only snow in a walkable cluster under the same gate.**

### PRIMARY: "[Brand] Home Care", one neighbourhood cluster

**Track A: residential cleaning (always on)**
- **Services:** flat-rate deep clean, move-in/move-out clean, standard clean. First-time clients are converted to biweekly recurring.
- **Channels:**
  - Door-knocking and door hangers in one cluster. These are not electronic messages, so CASL does not apply.
  - Kijiji, Facebook and Korean-community ads that the owner posts.
  - Replies to inbound inquiries (excluded from CASL s.6 by SOR/2013-221 s.3(b)).
  - CASL-vetted B2B emails to property managers and realtors, only if the owner's English is high.
- **Scope rules:**
  - Works in all five metros and without a car (transit-reachable jobs first).
  - **No key custody in month 1.** The client is present or a lockbox is the client's responsibility. Bonding norms: not researched.

**Track B: exterior (only after G1 / Gate S)**
- **Gutters:** hand and scoop only. The start around Oct 15 is **my assumption** from the "after leaves drop" source (F22). Paid on completion.
- **Snow:**
  - Walk-behind snowblower or shovel only, no truck.
  - **Contract season is Dec 1 – Mar 31.** A **per-visit rate applies to November storms** (default $60, chosen from the $50–150 band in F28).
  - Written terms include: Ontario 10-day cancellation text, a no-snow-onto-road clause, trigger depth, a low-snow clause, and the Nov-20 void condition.

### PARALLEL FLOOR
1. **EI.** File right after the last day of work, **no later than Sat Oct 10, 2026**. Declare all weekly net self-employment earnings. Apply the section 5.4 decision rule.
2. **TaskRabbit ($25 fee, reported) and Jiffy.** Categories: cleaning, furniture assembly, and TV mounting with no in-wall electrical. **Platform rates are client-facing "from" floors, not realized pay** (cleaning verifier). Demand for a new Tasker: **not found**.

### 5.4 EI vs business: the decision rule (ESTIMATE; assumes the maximum benefit and must be recomputed with the owner's actual benefit B)

- **EI at the maximum:** 729 × 52 ÷ 12 = **$3,159/month**. That is 55% of weekly insurable earnings, and the maximum applies only if insurable earnings are ≥ $68,900/yr (F5).
- **Path A example** (the activity is judged "minor extent"): $400 net in a week → deduction 50% × 400 = $200 → EI $529 → total **$929/week**. After CPP on the $400 (× 0.881): 529 + 352.40 = **$881.40/week**, about $3,816/month (× 4.33).
- **Path B** (full business): EI is $0 for each such week (F3). The business must beat Path A **after CPP**: net × 0.881 > 881.40, so **net > $1,000/week, about $4,332/month**.
  - The general form is: switch when business weekly net > (B − 0.5A + 0.881A) ÷ 0.881, where A is the Path A weekly net.
  - This ignores the $3,500 CPP exemption, which is the conservative choice.
- **Comparisons:**
  - B1 base October ($972.50) and November ($3,545 × 0.881 = $3,123) are both **below** Path A.
  - B2 base November operating ($7,110) is above it. B2 cash after snow capex ($4,811) is roughly level with it.

**Rule for week 1 (applies only if the owner is EI-eligible):**
1. File by Sat Oct 10. Phone Service Canada: describe Path A (hours, capital) and ask whether it would be treated as "minor extent".
2. **Default in October = Path A.** Build assets. Take platform and a limited number of declared direct jobs. Keep doing the job search required by s.18(1)(a).
3. **Switch to Path B** when the **trailing-2-week completed plus booked-forward net** is ≥ **$1,000/week** (recomputed with the actual B). In B2 this can happen for the gutter peak, but only if the booked November gutter jobs clear the threshold.
4. Declare every dollar either way (F4).

If the owner is **not** EI-eligible (unknown hours, reason for separation or severance, F6), run Path B from day 1.

## 11. Anticipated owner questions

1. **Can I make $5,000 in October?**
   No.
   - October base is $972.50 (B1) or −$220 cash / $2,230 operating (B2).
   - October conservative is −$256 (B1) or −$1,741 (B2).
   - Base first reaches about $5k/month in January 2027 (B1). B2 base reaches it on an operating basis in November and in cash from December.
   - Conservative never reaches $5k solo this winter.
2. **Should I claim EI?**
   Yes, if eligible: file by **Sat Oct 10** (F1). In October, EI plus a small declared side activity (about $929/week in the example) beats every business scenario. Switch to the full business only when it clears about $1,000/week net at the maximum benefit (5.4). Service Canada decides what counts as "minor extent".
3. **Is exterior work worth it if I have a truck?**
   Only modestly. In base, it adds about $3,000 over Oct–Mar (5.3). In conservative, gutters only recover their setup cost. Do it only under the gates and purchase triggers.
4. **Do I need to charge HST now?**
   No. At base volume, from about May 2027 (5.5). Track it by quarter.
5. **Do I need to register a business name?**
   The Ontario rules were not verified. Check with ServiceOntario before printing a brand name.
6. **I have a work permit. Can I do this?**
   The research did not cover it. Check your permit conditions or ask IRCC before any sale.
7. **No car?**
   B1 only, with a slower ramp. Shovel-only snow is possible if written insurance is obtained.
8. **Why not holiday lights?**
   The booking window is closing (F33), prices conflict 2–3x, it involves ladder work in winter, and the January takedown is unpaid.
9. **Can I take snow money now?**
   No. Nothing is collected before Dec 1. Ontario door-signed contracts carry a 10-day cancellation (F32), and GTA customers have been burned by prepayment (CP24).
10. **Is door-knocking legal?**
    CASL does not cover it. Ontario direct-agreement rules apply (F32). Municipal soliciting bylaws were **not researched**.
11. **What will insurance cost?**
    Unknown for your exact work. Snippet floors are about $450–500/yr (cleaning or window). Snow is $450–3,000, or about $1,800 for a handyperson (F23, F27, F29). You need written quotes in week 1.
12. **Will TaskRabbit give me work quickly?**
    Unknown. Approval time and new-Tasker demand were not found, and applications may not be processed at all (F21).
13. **Is the website free to host commercially?**
    Cloudflare's limits are official (F39). Free-plan commercial use is secondary-sourced only (F40). Read the terms at sign-up.
14. **What about the tool-deals site?**
    No priced query in any daily snapshot since 2026-08-08 (12 priced on 2026-08-07; F44). It gets archived in build item 0.
