## 2. Starting rate guidance

### 2.1 What the numbers mean

**Platform rates are client-facing "from" floors, not realized pay** (memo section 2, cleaning verifier). A "from $35/hr" page tells clients the cheapest listed price. It does not say what you will earn per week, and it does not say how many jobs you will get.

| Category | Researched floor | Status |
|---|---|---|
| One-time cleaning, Toronto (TaskRabbit) | "from $35/hr" | SNIPPET / CORRECTED: "a marketing floor, not an earnings benchmark" (memo F19, [taskrabbit.ca](https://www.taskrabbit.ca/locations/toronto/one-time)) |
| House cleaning, Toronto (TaskRabbit) | "prices begin at $40" | SNIPPET (cleaning dossier, same page family) |
| TV mounting (TaskRabbit) | Vancouver "starting at $45/hr". **Toronto: not found.** | SNIPPET ([taskrabbit.ca Vancouver](https://www.taskrabbit.ca/locations/vancouver/wall-mount-tv)) |
| Furniture assembly (TaskRabbit, Canada) | **Not found.** A "$34 on average" figure exists, but it comes from the **US** cost guide. **Do not use it.** | CORRECTED ([US guide](https://www.taskrabbit.com/cost-guides/assemble-furniture)) |
| IKEA flat-rate assembly tasks | The platform sets the price: about $90–110 for a 2-frame PAX wardrobe, with a 40-minute minimum paying at least $32.26 | third-party blog, unverifiable ([betterpayjobs](https://www.betterpayjobs.ca/taskrabbit-canada-earnings-ikea/), [TaskRabbit IKEA help](https://support.taskrabbit.com/hc/en-ca/articles/360012457092-How-Does-Payment-on-IKEA-Tasks-Work)) |
| "New Taskers charge $15–25/hr" | US affiliate blogs, no method shown | **UNVERIFIED**, near-zero weight (memo F20) |

### 2.2 What to set

1. **Set each category at the floor your own screen shows for your city when you sign up** (memo item 8: "starting rate at the category floor"). The table above comes from unopened snippets. What the platform shows you on screen is the real source. Write it down with the date.
2. **Do not go below the floor on day 1.** The only evidence that new Taskers charge less comes from unverified US blogs, and **nothing shows that a lower rate brings more jobs.** Review it at the day-14 check (Sun Oct 11) using your own numbers: requests received, jobs done, hours.
3. **Planning figures (ESTIMATE, memo section 5.1):**
   - Base: $35/h floor − 10% for supplies and transit = **$31.50/h**.
   - Conservative: $25/h − 10% = **$22.50/h**.
   - Jiffy: the commission comes out of the job payment. At a $35/h client price you keep about **$24.50–30.80/h** before your own costs (ESTIMATE: 35 × 0.70 to 35 × 0.88, commission unknown within 12–30%).
   - The memo counts platform hours in forecasts **only after approval arrives** (section 7).
4. **Your direct-client price book (`config/prices.ts`) is separate.** Do not copy flat prices from the price book into a platform profile, and do not quote platform hourly rates to your own direct clients.
5. **Taking platform clients off the platform** (sharing your phone, website or e-Transfer details) is governed by each platform's terms, which were **not researched.** Read them before you share `{PHONE}` or `{SITE}` with a platform client. **Leave contact details, your brand name and links out of the bio.**
6. **EI (if you claim it):** declare platform earnings every week. Declare net (gross minus operating expenses), allocated to the week the work was done (memo F4). Declare every dollar.
7. **GST/HST:** whether platform receipts count toward your $30,000 small-supplier test was **not verified** (memo section 5.5). Record them by calendar quarter anyway.

## 3. Skills and scope

Memo categories: **cleaning, furniture assembly, and TV mounting with no in-wall electrical** (memo section 2).

| Offer | Do | Refuse |
|---|---|---|
| Cleaning | Standard, deep and move-out cleans, following your written checklist (build item 14). | Biohazard or anything you are not equipped for (your judgment). |
| Furniture assembly | Flat-pack beds, desks, dressers, shelves, tables, using your own tools. | Anything that needs electrical or plumbing hook-up. |
| TV mounting | Mounting a TV on a bracket the client supplies or has approved, on a wall you know how to mount on safely. Cables tidied **on the wall surface** (cord cover) or left as they are. | **Running wires or power inside the wall, moving or adding outlets, hard-wiring.** The regulated-trade boundary was not verified, so refuse these jobs (memo section 3). Also refuse walls or TV sizes you are not sure you can handle **alone**: no helpers in month 1 (memo section 3). |
| **Not offered** | — | Plumbing or gas (memo section 3); moving or junk removal (no truck; not a memo category); pressure washing in Toronto; ladder or exterior window work; holiday lights. |

Tools: tool costs were **not found** in the research. Buy only what your first booked job needs.
Insurance: ask your broker whether the cleaning liability policy also covers platform jobs, assembly and TV mounting. TaskRabbit's own protection terms were not verified (labour dossier).

## 4. Bios and skill pitches (EN) with Korean explanations

**Rules for every text below:**
- `{FIRST_NAME}`: your first name as shown on the platform.
- `{AREA}`: your cluster or the areas you choose on screen.
- `{SUPPLIES_LINE}`: choose one and delete the other. "I bring my own cleaning supplies." / "I use your cleaning supplies and bring my own cloths."
- `{TRUE_EXPERIENCE_SENTENCE}`: write **one true sentence** about your real experience, or delete the line. Never invent years, job counts or reviews.
- `{LANGUAGES}`: "Korean" or "Korean and English". List English only if you can handle client messages in English. The AI can draft English replies, but you send them.
- If the platform has a length limit, cut from the bottom up. Keep the "I don't do electrical work" line.

## 5. Photo checklist (profile)

The platforms' photo rules were **not researched.** Follow the instructions on screen. These are suggestions:

- [ ] **Profile photo:** your face, alone, in daylight, against a plain background, with no sunglasses or hat, wearing plain or branded work clothes.
- [ ] **Tools:** your drill, level, stud finder and hand tools laid out on a table.
- [ ] **Cleaning kit:** vacuum, microfibre cloths, bucket and bottles.
- [ ] **Your own work, at your own home:** furniture you assembled, or a TV you mounted, in your own home.
- [ ] **Never** use photos of a client's home without their separate written photo opt-in (memo section 6). Never use internet or stock photos, or photos of work you did not do.
- [ ] Hide house numbers, mail, documents, screens and other people's faces.
- [ ] ID photos for verification: exactly as the platform asks.

## 6. First-week checklist (Mon Sep 28 – Sun Oct 4)

### Day 1, Mon Sep 28
- [ ] **Gate 0:** confirm from your own IRCC documents that you may be self-employed in Canada. If not, stop (memo section 2).
- [ ] Open the TaskRabbit **fee** and **requirements** pages yourself (links in section 1). Confirm the $25 fee and the requirements on the live page.
- [ ] Have ready: SIN, government photo ID, a Canadian **chequing** account, a credit/debit card, a phone number and an email address.
- [ ] Apply to **TaskRabbit** with three categories (cleaning, furniture assembly, TV mounting), and pay the $25.
- [ ] Apply to **Jiffy** as a pro. Before accepting, read the commission terms on screen and write the rate down.
- [ ] Record both application dates and every status change (CRM or a note).

### Days 2–7
- [ ] Check email and both apps every day for background-check or ID steps, and act on them the same day.
- [ ] Prepare the bio text (section 4) and photos (section 5), ready to paste the moment you are let in.
- [ ] Read each platform's terms on: sharing contact details, off-platform payment, cancellations, damage protection and insurance. Write the answers down.
- [ ] In the broker calls you are already making (memo day 2), ask whether the policy covers platform jobs, furniture assembly and TV mounting.
- [ ] Tools: check what you already own. Tool costs were not found, so buy only for a booked job.
- [ ] Set up a weekly record of platform earnings and expenses for EI declarations (memo F4) and for the finance tracker (build item 15).

### After approval
- [ ] Set rates at the on-screen category floor (section 2.2) and write down the date and the rates.
- [ ] Accept jobs you can reach by transit first (memo section 2).
- [ ] After each job, ask for a review **only through the platform's own review system.** Offer no discounts or gifts for reviews. Platform review rules were not researched, so stay on the safe side.

### Day-14 check (Sun Oct 11), memo section 8
- **No platform approval yet:** remove platform hours from every forecast, and do not re-apply elsewhere expecting speed.
- **Approved:** compare hours worked and money received with the memo's October base (15 platform hours), and keep going.
