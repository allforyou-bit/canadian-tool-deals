# Fixed-scope build agreement

One page. Plain language. Send it as the body of an email before you start, and
treat a written "yes, go ahead" as acceptance — that is enough for work this size,
and a signature ceremony for CAD 300 loses more deals than it protects.

Replace the bracketed parts. Delete this line and everything above it.

---

**Between:** [YOUR LEGAL NAME] ("I", "me") and [CLIENT NAME / COMPANY] ("you").
**Dated:** [DATE]

## What I will deliver

A one-page website for [YOUR OFFER IN ONE LINE], consisting of:

- One page, with copy written from the brief you send me
- Your own checkout, booking or contact link wired to the buttons
- A privacy page and a terms page
- Deployment to a live URL, hosted on a free plan that permits commercial use
- One round of changes (see below)
- The source files and deploy configuration, handed to you on payment

**Add-ons agreed:** [none / Ship Two, second page or Korean version, +CAD 150 /
Rush 24-hour turnaround, +CAD 150]

## What I will not deliver

Logo or brand identity design; photography or purchased stock imagery; a product
catalogue, shopping cart, inventory or order management; user accounts, a login or
a database; email hosting; ongoing edits after the included round; copywriting for
pages beyond the agreed count; migration of an existing site's content; SEO work
beyond the page's own tags; and any advice that is legally regulated — immigration,
legal, tax, medical or financial.

If you want any of it, it is a separate quote, not a scope argument.

## Timeline

48 hours from a **complete brief**, meaning: what you sell, who buys it, the
prices, your checkout or booking link, and any logo or photographs you want used.
The clock starts when the last of those arrives, not when the conversation starts.

## Price and payment

**CAD [300]**, flat. No tax is charged: I am a small supplier under the Canada
Revenue Agency threshold and am not registered for GST/HST.

Payment is due **on go-live** — after the page is on a live URL and you have seen
it. Interac e-Transfer. No deposit is taken.

Until payment, the page sits on my subdomain. On payment it points at your domain
if you have one, and the source and deploy configuration come to you.

## Changes

One round is included: one message listing what you want changed. Wording, prices,
section order, colour, contact details. Turned round the same day where it arrives
before 6pm.

A second round, or changes that alter the agreed structure, are quoted separately
at CAD [75] per round. I will tell you before doing the work, never after.

## Your checkout stays yours

The payment button on your page links to **your** payment account — your Stripe,
Square, Ko-fi, PayPal, Calendly or equivalent. My payment details never appear on
your page and I never handle your customers' money. Setting up that account, and
everything that follows from it, is yours.

## What you are responsible for

That you have the right to use the text, logo and photographs you send me, and
that what the page says about your business is true. I write from your brief; I
cannot verify your claims, your licences or your insurance.

## Ownership

On payment, the delivered page — its HTML, CSS, content and deploy configuration —
is yours outright, to change, move or hand to anyone else.

The generator used to produce it stays mine. You do not need it to own, host or
edit your page.

## If it goes wrong

**Before payment:** if you look at the finished page and do not want it, say so.
You pay nothing, nothing was published under your name, and we part on good terms.

**After payment:** the work is delivered and cannot be returned, which is why the
order is that way round. If something is broken — a link that does not work, a
price that is wrong, a page that will not load — tell me and I fix it, at no
charge, because it was never finished.

**Liability:** my total liability under this agreement is limited to the amount you
have paid me under it. Nothing here limits any right you have that cannot lawfully
be limited.

## Law

Ontario, Canada.
